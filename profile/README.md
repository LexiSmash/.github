## What is LexiSmash

LexiSmash is a real-time multiplayer word game, made to be played in a
group — live in the same room on a shared device, or remotely, each
player from their own phone or computer. One player creates a room
with a code, the others join, and together they build valid words to
score points.

Not just a solitary puzzle: there's chat, there's music, there's
someone trying to cheat — and the game notices.

## Highlights

- 🌍 **Six genuinely complete languages** — Italian, English, French,
  German, Spanish and Dutch each have their own dictionary, their own
  scoring system and their own game alphabet.
- 🎉 **A real hangout-with-friends atmosphere** — chat with a
  profanity filter, a jukebox with YouTube search and music synced
  across every player in the room, dynamic backgrounds, 30+ avatars.
- ⚖️ **Fairness between players** — a karma system tracks behavior
  over time, with real consequences for anyone repeatedly disrupting
  matches.
- 🏆 **Progress that lasts beyond a single match** — friends,
  achievements, XP/levels and a persistent cross-match leaderboard.
- ♿ **Accessible for different ways of playing** — voice control,
  gamepad/controller support, configurable keyboard shortcuts.
- 🤖 **AI-powered bots** to fill rooms that are too empty, with
  behavior designed to feel plausible.
- 📲 **Installable and playable offline** — as an app (PWA) on phone,
  tablet or computer, with full matches against bots even without a
  connection.
- 🏅 **Tools for running real tournaments** among friends or within a
  community, with automatically computed standings and brackets.

## 📚 The dictionary is open source

The lists of valid words for each language live in a separate public
repository: [LexiSmash_dictionaries](https://github.com/LexiSmash/LexiSmash_dictionaries).
A word dictionary isn't a trade secret — words are missing, some are
wrong, and anyone can propose a correction with a simple Pull Request.
The game still uses its own protected copy of the dictionary, so
players can't "download the answers."
